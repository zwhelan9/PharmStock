The Problem

Pharmacy Inventory Challenges

Manual stock management in pharmacies leads to costly, preventable problems.

💸 Expired Medicine Waste

Medicines expiring on shelves represent direct financial loss and a patient safety risk. Without visibility into batch expiry dates, pharmacists can't act until it's too late.

📉 Stockouts

Running out of critical medicines forces patients to go elsewhere. Without demand forecasting, reorders are reactive rather than proactive — causing avoidable shortages.

📋 Manual Tracking Errors

Spreadsheet-based inventory is error-prone. Incorrect stock counts, missing batch records, and delayed entries create a false picture of available stock.

🔄 No FEFO Enforcement

Without First-Expired-First-Out guidance, staff may dispense newer stock while older batches expire — accelerating waste and financial loss.

07 / 10
Module 3

⏰ Expiry Tracking

Four-tier alert system that flags batches from 90 days before expiry, with financial loss estimation and FEFO guidance.

🗑️ Expired — Batch past expiry date, still active in stock
🚨 Critical — Expires within 30 days
⚠️ Warning — Expires in 31–60 days
👁️ Watch — Expires in 61–90 days
  • ✓ Estimated financial loss per expired batch
  • ✓ Expiry log for removed / returned / donated batches
  • ✓ 3-month calendar view grouped by month
  • ✓ FEFO (First Expired, First Out) batch ordering

Module 4

📈 Demand Forecasting

Machine learning models trained on 180 days of historical sales data to predict future demand with confidence intervals.

History Available Model Used Confidence
< 7 days Global Average ~50%
7 – 29 days Weighted Moving Average + Trend ~65%
30+ days Linear Regression + Day-of-Week 55–95%
  • ✓ 7, 14, and 30-day forecast horizons
  • ✓ Per-day predicted quantity with upper/lower bounds
  • ✓ Automatic stockout day prediction
  • ✓ Reorder recommendation list for all medicines
